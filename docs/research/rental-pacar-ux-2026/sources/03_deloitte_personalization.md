# Source 03 — Deloitte Digital Personalization

- URL: https://www.deloittedigital.com/content/dam/digital/us/documents/insights/insights-20240610-personalization-report.pdf
- Type: industry consumer research
- Scores: credibility medium; recency medium; bias medium

## Verbatim evidence

> “Around half of surveyed Gen Zers and millennials deemed personalized experiences very or extremely important.”

> “Gen Zers were more influenced by social media and creator / influencer recommendations. Meanwhile, millennials were more likely to be influenced by search engines, social media and customer reviews / ratings.”

## Design implication

Let people browse by the moment they need help with—event, makan, jalan, ngobrol—rather than forcing them to decode an abstract marketplace. Profile cards should expose vibe, context, and social proof quickly.
