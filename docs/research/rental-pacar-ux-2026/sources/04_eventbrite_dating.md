# Source 04 — Eventbrite Dating Report

- URL: https://mms.businesswire.com/media/20240606525971/en/2153130/1/Eventbrite_2024_Dating_Report_Final.pdf
- Type: industry audience research
- Scores: credibility medium; recency medium; bias medium

## Verbatim evidence

> “A significant number of both Gen Z (45%) and Millennials (35%) find one of the biggest challenges of online dating to be connecting with others who share their interests.”

> “Among those who prefer dating in person, 41% of Gen Z and 27% of Millennials say safety is one of their top concerns when meeting someone online.”

> “More than half of daters, including 54% of Gen Z and 53% of Millennials, say it’s hard to start or continue meaningful conversations online.”

## Design implication

Show shared context and conversation starters in the product UI. PacaRent should sell a specific experience, not just a face in a card.
