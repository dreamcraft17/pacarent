# Source 05 — Aalborg University Dating UX Thesis

- URL: https://projekter.aau.dk/it-s-a-match-investigating-user-experience-and-algorithmic-trust-in-dating-platforms-toward-inclusiv-299fa6de.html
- Type: academic thesis
- Scores: credibility medium; recency high; bias medium

## Verbatim evidence

> “Participants were frustrated by opaque matching—limited insight into how the algorithm pairs people—by little control over matching criteria, and by restricted options for representing identity.”

> “They asked for clearer explanations of platform intentions, improved onboarding for new users (a guided introduction), and more inclusive, flexible profile customization.”

## Design implication

Give users simple, explicit control over the kind of companion they need. Explain why a profile is relevant and make the service boundaries obvious before chat or booking.
