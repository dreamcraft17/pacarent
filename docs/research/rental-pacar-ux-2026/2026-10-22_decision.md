# PacaRent UX Direction — Gen Z + Millennials

*Generated: 2026-10-22 | Genre: decision brief*

## Bottom line

PacaRent should feel less like a directory of “pendamping” and more like a **personal moment marketplace**: “lo punya momen, kita cariin orang yang pas buat nemenin.” The visual language can be playful and expressive, but the decision path must feel fast, transparent, and safe.

## What to change

### 1. Make the hero feel like a personal prompt

- Replace generic activity-first framing with a direct need-state.
- Use a large prompt such as “Lagi butuh ditemenin?” and let the user choose a moment.
- Keep one dominant CTA: “Pilih yang nemenin gue”.

### 2. Turn categories into mood/moment chips

Use compact, tappable chips: `Acara`, `Makan`, `Jalan`, `Ngobrol`, `Foto`, `Biar nggak awkward`.

This makes personalization tangible and gives both Gen Z and Millennials an immediate route to relevance.

### 3. Make cards feel like people + context, not inventory

Each card should show: photo, name/city, verified status, a short “vibe” line, available context tags, starting price/hour, and one conversational CTA. Add one line such as “Cocok buat: nemenin acara / ngobrol santai”.

### 4. Separate playful discovery from serious trust

Use expressive accent colors, sticker-like tags, and friendly microcopy in discovery. Use calm, explicit modules for verified identity, public meeting guidance, price, cancellation, and report controls.

### 5. Reduce mobile cognitive load

Keep the hero, moment chips, and first profile cards discoverable without a long explanatory block. Move “cara kerja” and legal links below the primary decision path, but keep safety visible at the point of booking.

## Hypothesis verdicts

- H1: supported directionally by personalization research and audience research; validate locally with CTA test.
- H2: supported by relation-making and dating UX research; trust/safety must be visible, not hidden in S&K.
- H3: supported by mobile UX research; implement as compact overview + progressive disclosure.

## Recommended first experiment

Run a 2-cell hero test for 7 days:

- A: “Butuh temen? Lu sendiri?”
- B: “Lagi butuh ditemenin?”

Keep the same visual system and measure profile CTA click-through, first chat start, and S&K/safety engagement. Do not optimize only for button clicks; a playful headline that creates low-quality chats is not a win.

## Confidence

Medium. The cross-source pattern is consistent, but the evidence is not a representative sample of Jabodetabek PacaRent users. Validate with 5–8 moderated usability sessions and a small A/B test before locking the direction.
