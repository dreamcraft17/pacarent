## Relevant evidence

> “Empty states provide opportunities for designers to communicate system status, increase learnability of the system, and deliver direct pathways for key tasks.”

> “Don’t let empty-state design be an afterthought.”

## Design implication

The no-inventory view should state what is happening, teach the booking flow in context, and link directly to a meaningful next action such as joining as a partner or switching discovery mode.
