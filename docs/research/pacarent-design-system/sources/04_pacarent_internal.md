## Relevant evidence

The existing brief defines the north star as completed booking with a verified partner, and the UX decision explicitly adapts card discovery toward “Tertarik → Booking,” not match/chat mechanics.

## Design implication

The visual system should use calm trust cues, concrete Bahasa Indonesia, and booking/calendar affordances. It should not use hearts, romantic matching language, or “who liked you” mechanics.
