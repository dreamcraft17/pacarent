# PacaRent design direction — 2026-10-17

## Direction

**Quiet confidence / editorial marketplace.** PacaRent uses warm off-white surfaces, deep ink typography, teal trust accents, soft borders, and deliberate spacing. It should feel human and local without looking like a generic SaaS dashboard or a romantic matching app.

## Design principles

1. **Show the next move.** Every empty or loading state answers “what can I do now?”
2. **Trust before charm.** Verification, scheduling, location, and payment cues come before decorative personality.
3. **One primary action.** Use a clear booking action and a quieter alternative; avoid competing CTAs.
4. **Responsive by composition.** Desktop gets a two-column discovery layout; mobile gets a single focus with persistent bottom navigation.
5. **Accessible by default.** Keep controls at least 44px where possible, retain visible focus, and provide non-drag equivalents.

## Decision status

- Confirmed: new app-bar, sidebar, empty-state composition, filter treatment, and booking-oriented visual vocabulary.
- Under-determined: final photography art direction and whether users prefer card or list as the default on desktop.

## Adversarial review

- Risk: the editorial look can feel too premium if copy does not stay concrete. Mitigation: use short Indonesian labels and visible booking metadata.
- Risk: a three-step empty state can become onboarding clutter. Mitigation: keep it inline, compact, and removable once inventory is live.
- Risk: teal-only hierarchy can flatten the page. Mitigation: use ink, cream, and muted border tokens for layers; reserve teal for actions and trust.

## Next validation

Moderated remote usability test with 5 users, 15–20 minutes each. Task: “Kamu ingin ditemani ke acara minggu depan. Cari mitra yang cocok dan jelaskan langkah berikutnya.” Success: 4/5 users identify a next action and understand that PacaRent ends in a booking, not a match.
