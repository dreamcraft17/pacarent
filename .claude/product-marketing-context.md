## Customer language (verbatim / target)

- "Minus one / plus one error 404"
- "Kondangan tp gapunya pacar"
- "Bukan swipe — langsung booking"
- "Vibes cocok" (bukan "mitra" / "layanan")

## Words to use

- kamu, yuk, gak/gak perlu, langsung, verified, jadwal, tempat public
- Aku tertarik · booking, Daftar — kabarin aku

## Words to avoid

- mitra (UI), seamless, inovatif, optimal
- janji jodoh / match / swipe romantis
- copy korporat: "pendamping terverifikasi dengan alur yang jelas" tanpa human touch

## Brand voice

- Tone: **kakak/kakak digital** — Bumble warmth + Tinder pep-talk, TanTan simple
- DO: second person, short lines, one reassurance per screen
- DON'T: bullet korporat, terlalu banyak "→", shouting (!!!)

## Differentiation vs Tinder/Bumble/Tantan

- **They:** mutual like → chat → date ambiguity  
- **PacaRent:** tertarik → **tanggal & lokasi** → booking; bukan cari jodoh
